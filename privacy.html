Privacy Policy

Snap Translator · Effective 11 November 2026

Snap Translator translates text, speech and photos. This policy explains what happens to the things you give the app. The short version: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your history stays on your iPhone. Text is sent to a translation service only when it has to be translated online.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your translation history, favourites and settings.
  • Photos you take or choose. Text in a photo is recognised on the device, and the photo itself is never uploaded.
  • Translations made with downloaded offline languages, and translations made with On-Device AI (Apple Intelligence).
  • Speech, when you translate offline (on-device speech recognition).

What is sent to a service, and why

WhatSent toWhy

Text is sent only to produce the result you asked for. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data about you. These providers process it under their own terms: Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

The Snap Translator keyboard

Without Full Access, the keyboard works entirely on your iPhone and sends nothing. With Full Access turned on, it sends only the text you choose to translate or rewrite, and only when you tap Translate or a rewrite tool. It does not record or send anything else you type. Full Access also lets the keyboard share settings, such as your languages and theme, with the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have Pro.

Permissions

  • Camera: to capture text to translate.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: to translate what you say.
  • Photos (add only): to save a translated photo when you ask.

Each permission is asked for the first time you use the feature, and you can change it any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Tracking and children

Snap Translator does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and shows no ads. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can delete your history in Settings › Clear History, or remove all app data by deleting the app. Because there is no account, we hold no profile about you to delete.
