Help

Answers to the questions people ask most.

Add the Snap Translator keyboard

  1. Open the Settings app › General › Keyboard › Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard and choose Snap Translator.
  3. In any app, hold the globe key and pick Snap Translator.

Why does the keyboard ask for Full Access?

Typing works without it. Translating and rewriting need Full Access, because iOS lets a keyboard reach the internet only with it. The keyboard sends only the text you choose to translate or rewrite. To turn it on: Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Snap Translator › Allow Full Access.

Translate without internet

Open Settings in the app › Offline Languages and download the languages you need, then turn on the offline switch. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, On-Device AI also works offline.

Restore a purchase on a new iPhone

Open any Pro page and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

Cancel your subscription

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Snap Translator › Cancel Subscription. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Free AI translations

Without Pro you get 4 AI Lite translations every 24 hours, counted from the first one. When they are used, the app shows how long until they come back. Deleting and reinstalling the app does not reset them. Standard and On-Device AI translations are free without a daily limit.
